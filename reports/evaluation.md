## Rules

- **Scored only where the clerk typed the field.** GSTIN blank = blank counts as correct, on claims where the clerk typed the other fields. The 6 queried claims with nothing typed are not scored.
- **total_amount** ★ exact to the paisa; also within Rs 1 and within Rs 10 (the policy's rounding).
- **receipt_date** ★ exact date. The clerk's date is parsed day-first, MM/DD only if DD/MM is impossible or after submission.
- **receipt_number** ★ exact after removing spaces; also case-insensitive.
- **gstin** ★ exact after case/space clean-up and O/0, I/1 fixes on both sides.
- **vendor** ★ fuzzy: rapidfuzz token_sort_ratio >= 90 on lower-cased text without punctuation (the clerk alone has ~117 spellings of ~70 vendors); also exact after that normalisation.
- ★ = headline metric. **Overall** = all ★ field checks pooled.
- **Adjudicated**: where the photo shows the clerk was wrong, the photo's value is the truth; 'ambiguous' mismatches are dropped. The vs-clerk numbers are never changed.

### Rupees paid wrongly, and what the checks catch

Claims our checks send to review for a repeat bill, an altered amount or a claim above the bill. A repeat counts in full; an altered or over-claimed bill counts the excess over the real amount (from the amount in words or the printed figure; a leading "1" added to a three-digit bill counts Rs 1,000).

|---|---|---|---|---|

| Rs 2,000 or more | repeat of an earlier bill (whole amount) | 1 | Rs 2,400 | Rs 2,400 |
| under Rs 2,000 | altered: amount in words | 2 | Rs 2,000 | Rs 2,000 |
| under Rs 2,000 | altered: leading 1 added (estimate Rs 1,000) | 1 | Rs 1,000 | Rs 1,000 |
